Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how GMRP Central (“the bot”) may handle information when used in the Greater Melbourne Roleplay (GMRP) Discord server.

This policy describes intended handling practices. Before public launch, the bot’s actual storage, logging, hosting and retention settings must be checked and this policy updated to match them.

Information that may be processed

To provide tickets and moderation-support workflows, the bot may process Discord user IDs, server and channel IDs, usernames or display names, ticket messages, ticket categories, timestamps, and actions taken through bot commands or buttons. Information depends on which features are enabled.

How information is used

Information is used to create and manage support tickets, route requests to authorised staff, handle appeals or reports, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain the bot’s security.

Sharing and access

Ticket contents may be visible to the person who opened the ticket and authorised GMRP staff with access to that ticket. Information is not intended to be sold or used for advertising. Hosting providers or service providers may process technical data needed to operate the bot.

Storage and retention

Retention depends on the bot’s deployed configuration. Ticket records and logs should only be kept for as long as needed for support, moderation, security or legitimate record-keeping. Do not assume messages are permanently deleted when a ticket channel is closed unless the deployed system confirms this.

Your choices and requests

For questions or requests about information handled by GMRP Central, contact the GMRP server staff through the official GMRP Discord. Do not send passwords, payment details or other highly sensitive information in a support ticket.

Children and Discord

Users must meet Discord’s minimum age requirements and follow Discord’s terms and applicable laws in their region.

Changes

This policy may be updated when bot features or data practices change. The revision date above will be updated accordingly.
